# Motion Prompting

Describe motion as a sequence, not only as a visual style.

## Structure

Subject + starting state + movement + camera + timing + environment response + ending state.

## Example

> Product centered on a clean surface. Camera begins in a wide locked shot, slowly pushes forward, product rotates slightly, soft reflections move across the surface, background remains stable, movement settles into a clean final frame.

## Control rules

- Specify one primary camera movement.
- Specify what moves and what stays fixed.
- Give the motion a beginning and ending state.
- Use timing words such as slow, brisk, sudden, continuous, or rhythmic when the model supports them.
- Avoid contradictory motion instructions.
